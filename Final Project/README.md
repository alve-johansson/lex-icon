# Hotel California Booking System

"The most important things are the hardest to say because words diminish them." 

Welcome to the Hotel California Booking System.

Hotels can be very hot. The AC's somethimes broken. In the winter, the case is often inversed. We have fixed the heater but don't expect the cold showers to ever become anything close to soothing.
Hotels can stay the same. They can be very expensive and sometimes clean. Some of them are close to cities, some of them are far away. Some of them have room-service. They might let you order a bottle of tequila and encounter the sublime. It is strictly forbidden use any kind of door-dash style food delivery service to the hotel's perimeter.

To book a room, one has to first look at the world with an open mind. What is possible to do in this confined space? Is it possible for the inside of the hotel to supereseed the limits of linear space?

Don't wait for too long to book your room, we fill up fast. This booking system seems to operate outside of the regular continuum. Expect som noumenal oddities. Extended stays will bring down instance price. Talk to the manager.

This python script is a part of my education as a developer and is not intended to be used for any real or live case. It's just for exploring basic python and OOP structures.
