# Hotel California Booking System

"The most important things are the hardest to say because words diminish them." 

Welcome to the Hotel California Booking System.

Hotels can be very hot. The AC's somethimes broken. In the winter, the case is often inversed. We fixed the heater but never expect the cold shower to become anything close to soothing.
Hotels can stay the same. They can be very expensive and sometimes clean. Some of them are close to cities, some of them are far away. Some of them have room-service. They let you order tequila and encounter the sublime. It is not allowed to use any kind of door-dash service.

To book a room, one has to first look at the world with an open mind. What is possible in this confined space? Can you share your space with only yourself?

Sometimes there's no need for time to exist. This booking system seems to operate outside of the regular continuum. Expect som noumenal oddities. 

This python script is a part of my education as a developer and is not intended to be used for any real or live case. It's just for exploring basic python and OOP structures.
